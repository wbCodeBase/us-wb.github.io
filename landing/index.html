FAQ

Questions business owners ask us.

Is the marketing audit really free?

Yes. There's no cost and no obligation. You'll get a written audit with specific recommendations you can use whether or not you work with us.

How much do I need to spend on Google Ads?

It depends on your industry and service area. During the audit we'll estimate a realistic starting budget based on local search volume and competition, so you can decide with real numbers.

How long until I see results?

Google Ads can start producing calls within the first few weeks once campaigns are live. Local SEO builds over time, and most businesses see meaningful movement in 3–6 months. We'll set clear expectations for your market up front.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. We work month-to-month. If we're not delivering value, you shouldn't be stuck with us.

Who owns the ad accounts and website?

You do. Your Google Ads account, Google Business Profile, analytics and website stay in your name. If you ever leave, everything stays with you.

What does "AI-powered" actually mean here?

We use AI where it genuinely helps: responding to new leads quickly, spotting wasted ad spend, analyzing call quality and making sure your business shows up in AI search tools. A human strategist reviews and owns every decision.

Do you work with businesses in my state?

Yes. We work with local businesses across the United States, remotely, with scheduled calls in your time zone.
